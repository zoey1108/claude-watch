Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing AI 一点通. Please find the requested information below. A screen recording captured on a physical Apple Watch is attached.

1. Screen recording
The attached video was recorded on a physical Apple Watch Series 11 running the latest watchOS. It starts with launching the app and shows the typical flow: tapping to see the next tip, rotating the Digital Crown to go back, long-pressing to favorite a tip, opening the Favorites list, and changing the daily reminder setting. The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content, and no paid content or features inside the app (it is a one-time paid download with no In-App Purchases).

2. Purpose and target audience
AI 一点通 is a standalone Apple Watch app that teaches practical tips for using AI assistants, one short card at a time. It is for Chinese-speaking students, office workers and developers who use AI assistants such as Claude and want to learn better prompting techniques, keyboard shortcuts and features in spare moments. It solves the problem that useful tips are scattered across long documentation and are easily forgotten; the app turns them into 90 bite-sized cards that can be read at a glance on the wrist, plus optional daily reminders that help users build the habit.

3. Setup and main features
No setup, login, credentials or sample files are required. Open the app on Apple Watch:
- Tap anywhere on the card to show the next tip.
- Rotate the Digital Crown to go back to previously viewed tips.
- Long-press a card to add or remove it from Favorites (a confirmation toast appears).
- Tap the star icon (top left) to open Favorites.
- Tap the gear icon (top right) to choose 0–3 daily tip reminders (local notifications).
- Optionally add the "AI 小技巧" complication to a watch face or the Smart Stack.

4. External services
None. The app works entirely offline. All 90 tips are bundled inside the app, and it makes no network requests. It does not use any data providers, authentication services, payment processors, analytics, advertising SDKs or AI services. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

5. Regional differences
The app functions identically in all regions where it is available. The content is in Simplified Chinese. The app is not offered in mainland China.

6. Regulated industries and third-party material
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All tip content was written by the developer. "Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC; the app mentions it only descriptively, to explain which product some tips are about. The app does not use Anthropic's logos or brand assets, its name does not include any third-party trademark, and both the app description and the support page state that it is an independent, unofficial learning tool not affiliated with Anthropic.
